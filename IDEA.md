## Concept ##

A self hosted open source rival to Hello Fresh, or other meal prep services. Instead of paying a company to send you all the groceries you need to make a meal, you share recipes or videos with open-fresh, it then builds you a meal plan and a shopping list based on the meals you want.

The user create a kitchen, inside the kitchen is a pantry, a fridge, a freezer, a range, and a cookbook. The user can add recipes to the cookbook by adding URLs to webpages or videos. The user can specify how many meals they want planned per week, and a rough budget on how much they want to spend on those meals, they can then select meals directly from the cookbook and add them to the calendar, or they can have the calendar auto populate with meals from the cookbook. 

The grocery list looks at the calendar and pulls the recipes the user has chosen or have auto populate and generates a grocery list based on the meals on the calendar.

The grocery list, the budget and the calendar are all correlated.

### Features ###

  * Recipe Discovery
  * Automated Meal Planning
  * Automated shopping list generation
  * Budget Setting
  * Social Media recipe video ingest
  * Webpage recipe ingest


### Interface ###

  * React front end (mobile & desktop friendly)
  * python back end
  * CLI
